# Nexisure General Coverage Exclusions & Fraud Provisions

## Section 1: Non-Negotiable Coverage Exclusions
Across all policy tiers (Third-Party, Comprehensive, and Zero Depreciation), Nexisure shall NOT be liable to indemnify any claim arising directly or indirectly from:
1. **Intoxication & Substance Impairment**: Any accident occurring while the driver of the vehicle is under the influence of intoxicating alcohol, narcotics, drugs, or hallucinogens above the legal blood-alcohol limit.
2. **Unlicensed or Disqualified Operation**: Operating the vehicle without a valid, subsisting driver's license, or driving while disqualified from holding a license.
3. **Usage Beyond Limitations**: Using a private personal vehicle for hire, reward, rideshare fare hauling, racing, pace-making, speed testing, or unauthorized commercial delivery.
4. **Gradual Deterioration & Mechanical Breakdown**: Wear, tear, rusting, corrosion, mechanical or electrical breakdown, failure, or breakage not resulting from an external collision or violent accident.
5. **Consequential Losses**: Consequential losses including car rental expenses during repair downtime, depreciation in market resale value, or loss of business earnings.
6. **Deliberate or Malicious Acts by Policyholder**: Intentional destruction, staged accidents, or willful neglect to safeguard the vehicle after an initial event.
7. **War, Terrorism & Nuclear Radiation**: Damages arising directly or indirectly from war, invasion, civil commotion, foreign enemy hostilities, ionizing radiation, or nuclear contamination.

## Section 2: Consequence of Misrepresentation
Any deliberate concealment of material facts, falsification of driver identity, inflated loss estimates, or forged invoices shall result in immediate claim forfeiture, policy cancellation without premium refund, and referral to the legal compliance registry.
