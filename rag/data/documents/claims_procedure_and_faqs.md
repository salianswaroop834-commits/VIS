# Nexisure Claims Adjudication Procedure, Documentation, and FAQs

## Section 1: Standard Claim Filing Procedure
Policyholders must follow this 4-step adjudication workflow:
1. **Immediate Notification**: Notify Nexisure within 48 hours of accident or theft via the online Customer Portal, mobile web app, or 24/7 claims hotline.
2. **Document Submission**: Upload the following mandatory documents:
   - Certificate of Insurance / Policy Number.
   - Driver's License of the person driving at the time of the incident.
   - Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC / Logbook).
   - Police First Information Report (FIR) - strictly required in all cases of vehicle theft, third-party injury, or major fatal accidents.
   - High-resolution photographic evidence of vehicle damage from 4 orthogonal angles (front, rear, both sides, and odometer).
3. **Surveyor Inspection**: Nexisure dispatches a certified independent surveyor to the workshop within 24 to 48 business hours to verify physical damages against the preliminary estimate.
4. **Adjudication & Settlement**:
   - **Cashless Settlement**: If repaired at a Nexisure Authorized Network Garage, the insurer settles approved repair invoices directly with the workshop minus the applicable deductible.
   - **Reimbursement Settlement**: If repaired at an out-of-network garage, the policyholder pays upfront and submits original bills and payment receipts for electronic reimbursement within 5 to 7 business days.

## Section 2: Frequently Asked Questions (Claims FAQs)
- **Q: Can I drive my car after an accident?**
  A: If the vehicle has suffered radiator puncture, oil pan damage, steering failure, or glass breakage, you must NOT drive it. Operating a severely damaged vehicle causes engine seizure which is categorized as consequential damage and excluded from claim payout. Utilize Nexisure Towing Assistance.
- **Q: How is the maximum payout calculated for a total loss?**
  A: For constructive total loss (where repair costs exceed 75% of IDV), the maximum compensation is the vehicle's Insured Declared Value (IDV) recorded in the policy schedule minus the salvage scrap value and compulsory deductible.
- **Q: What is the turnaround time for claim adjudication?**
  A: Standard claims are reviewed and approved or rejected within 3 business days of receiving the surveyor's final assessment report.
