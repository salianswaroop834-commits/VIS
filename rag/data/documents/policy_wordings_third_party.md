# Nexisure Statutory Third-Party Liability Policy Wording (Plan Code: THIRD_PARTY)

## Section 1: Statutory Mandate & Intent
The Nexisure Third-Party Liability Policy fulfills the compulsory legal requirements mandated by motor vehicle legislation. This policy does NOT provide any compensation or indemnity for damage or loss incurred by the policyholder's own vehicle.

## Section 2: Coverage Limits
1. **Third-Party Bodily Injury or Death**: Unlimited indemnity for legal liability determined by a court or competent motor accident claims tribunal for death or bodily injury caused to third-party pedestrians, vehicle passengers, or other road users.
2. **Third-Party Property Damage (TPPD)**: Legal indemnity for physical damage caused to third-party vehicles, street infrastructure, fences, or private properties up to a statutory maximum limit of $750,000 per accident.
3. **Legal Defense Costs**: Reasonable legal fees and legal representation costs incurred with prior written consent of Nexisure to defend civil litigation arising from covered accidents.

## Section 3: Absolute Exclusions for Third-Party Policies
1. Any physical damage, loss, scratch, collision, dent, or total loss of the insured vehicle itself.
2. Theft, burglary, or fire destruction of the insured vehicle.
3. Damage to personal belongings, gadgets, luggage, or tools carried inside the insured vehicle.
4. Accidents occurring while the vehicle is driven outside designated jurisdictional territories.
