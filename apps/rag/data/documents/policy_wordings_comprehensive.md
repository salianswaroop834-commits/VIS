# Nexisure Comprehensive Vehicle Insurance Policy Wording (Plan Code: COMPREHENSIVE)

## Section 1: Scope of Coverage
The Nexisure Comprehensive Vehicle Insurance Policy provides dual-layer protection covering:
1. **Own Damage (OD)**: Accidental loss or physical damage to the insured vehicle caused by collision, overturning, fire, explosion, self-ignition, lightning, burglary, housebreaking, theft, riot, strike, earthquake, flood, storm, cyclone, or malicious acts.
2. **Third-Party Liability (TP)**: Statutory indemnification for legal liability incurred by the policyholder arising out of bodily injury or death of third parties, as well as damage caused to third-party property up to policy statutory limits ($1,000,000).
3. **Personal Accident (PA) Cover**: Compulsory personal accident protection for the owner-driver up to $50,000 in the event of accidental death or permanent total disability.

## Section 2: Deductibles and Depreciation
1. **Standard Compulsory Deductible**: Every claim under Section 1 is subject to a mandatory compulsory deductible based on the vehicle value tier:
   - Vehicle IDV up to $25,000: Standard deductible is $500.
   - Vehicle IDV above $25,000: Standard deductible is $1,000.
2. **Voluntary Deductibles**: Policyholders who opt for a higher voluntary deductible receive an annual premium discount of 5% to 15%.
3. **Depreciation on Parts Replacement**:
   - Rubber, nylon, plastic parts, tyres, and tubes: 50% depreciation deduction.
   - Fiberglass components: 30% depreciation deduction.
   - Glass parts: Nil depreciation (100% reimbursed).
   - Metallic parts: Scaled depreciation between 0% (year 1) and 50% (over 10 years).

## Section 3: Geographic Limits & SLA
- Coverage applies within the continental territory and approved border crossings.
- Notice of accidental loss must be provided to Nexisure within 48 hours of occurrence.
- Cashless garage network access is guaranteed across all certified Nexisure partner service centers.
