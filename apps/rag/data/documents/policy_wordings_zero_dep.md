# Nexisure Zero Depreciation Premium Policy Wording (Plan Code: ZERO_DEP_PREMIUM)

## Section 1: Executive Rider Terms (Bumper-to-Bumper)
The Nexisure Zero Depreciation Premium Policy is our premier high-tier coverage plan designed for vehicle owners who require 100% replacement value reimbursement without customary depreciation deductions during claim settlement.

## Section 2: Zero Depreciation Benefits
1. **100% Depreciation Waiver**: In the event of a covered collision or accidental loss, Nexisure waives all standard depreciation deductions on:
   - Rubber parts, nylon parts, airbags, plastic fittings, mudguards, and bumpers (normally 50% deduction).
   - Fiberglass hoods, panels, and body components (normally 30% deduction).
   - Glass windshields, rear windows, and side mirrors (100% covered).
   - Metallic doors, roof, bonnet, and chassis components (100% covered without age degradation).
2. **Consumables Coverage Add-On**: Includes replacement cost of engine oil, coolant, lubricants, nuts, bolts, washers, and AC gas required in authorized repairs.
3. **Roadside Assistance (RSA) & Towing**: 24/7 unlimited roadside towing up to 100 miles to the nearest authorized service facility, flat-tire assistance, emergency fuel dispatch, and battery jump-start.

## Section 3: Eligibility and Claim Limits
1. **Vehicle Age Limitation**: Zero Depreciation coverage is strictly limited to vehicles aged 5 years or younger (0 to 60 months from date of original registration). Vehicles exceeding 5 years must transition to standard Comprehensive coverage.
2. **Claim Count Cap**: A maximum of two (2) Zero Depreciation claims may be registered per policy year. Any subsequent claims within the same policy year are settled under standard Comprehensive depreciation rates.
3. **Compulsory Deductible**: Standard policy deductible ($500) remains payable by the insured.
