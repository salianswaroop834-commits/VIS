Geographic Concentration

Metropolitan and Urban zones account for 64% of total claim volume and register loss ratios exceeding 150%, demonstrating prime candidacy for localized territorial rating factors.

Vehicles equipped with anti-theft devices consistently yield a 3.1–3.4% reduction in claim incidence across both locked garages and street parking, justifying premium discounts.

Average claims settlement cycle stands at 19.7 days with a 4.78% fraud flag rate, signaling efficient triage with opportunities for automated straight-through processing (STP).
