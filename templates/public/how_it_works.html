How Nexisure Protects Your Vehicle

A transparent, step-by-step walkthrough of our insurance lifecycle from quotation to settlement.

01

Get a Transparent Quote

Input vehicle specifications, model year, and declared value. Receive transparent rate calculations alongside an explainable coverage recommendation based on vehicle age and risk.

02

Simulated Checkout

Complete your policy issuance through our educational checkout simulator featuring Luhn checksum validation and instant policy generation with locked financial rates.

03

File a Claim Anytime

Register a claim through our customer portal or directly via the conversational AI assistant with explicit confirmation steps. Claims enter an unassigned shared queue.

04

Human Review & Settlement

A licensed claims handler self-assigns the claim, inspects damage evidence, reviews ML risk estimations as decision support, and authorizes final settlement or rejection.
