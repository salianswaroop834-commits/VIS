Platform Mission

Bridging Reliable Insurance Operations with Responsible AI

Nexisure Vehicle Insurance is an educational capstone platform engineered to demonstrate how modern machine learning, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and strict transactional guardrails can enhance insurance management without replacing human oversight.

Originally conceptualized from the core business workflows of the Star Protect Vehicle mini-project, Nexisure elevates vehicle underwriting, policy lifecycle servicing, and claims resolution into a cohesive, secure Python/Django architecture powered by Supabase PostgreSQL.

Responsible Principles

  • Human-in-the-Loop Decisions: No claim is ever automatically approved or rejected by an algorithm or LLM.
  • Evidence Grounding: AI answers are strictly anchored to verified knowledge documents with explicit citations.
  • Transparent Estimations: Predictive risk scores and claim severity values are labeled as probabilistic estimates rather than binding verdicts.
  • Strict Authorization: Supabase Row Level Security ensures cross-customer record leakage is impossible.
